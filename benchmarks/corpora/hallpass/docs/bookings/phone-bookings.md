# Taking a booking over the phone (front desk)

Written for reception staff at the leisure centres, 2023. Tom W.

Residents can always ring up instead of using the website. To book for them:

1. Open Staff → Diary and pick the facility and day.
2. Click an empty cell. (Grey striped = someone is in the middle of paying for
   it online; leave it alone, it'll either go green or free up again.)
3. Search for the resident by email or postcode. If they're not on the system,
   use "New resident" — you need at least a name and either an email or a
   phone number.
4. Choose "Pay now by card" or "Pay at desk".
   - **Pay now by card**: the system holds the slot for 10 minutes and sends
     a payment link by email. Read the amount to them. If they don't pay in
     10 minutes the slot is released.
   - **Pay at desk**: only if they're standing in front of you with the
     money. Take payment on the card machine first, *then* press Confirm.
5. Tell them the booking reference (starts with HP-B).

## Cancellations by phone

Find the booking, press Cancel, choose "Customer request". The refund is
worked out automatically depending on how much notice they gave — don't
promise a full refund over the phone.

If it's us cancelling (closure, double-booked by mistake, boiler broken) use
"Council cancellation" instead so they get everything back.

## Block bookings

Don't take these over the phone. Ask the club to email
bookings@ with the day, time, hall and dates and it goes to the bookings
officer.
