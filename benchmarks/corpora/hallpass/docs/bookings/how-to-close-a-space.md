# Closing a space or a whole building

aka adding a blackout / maintenance window. Staff → Closures → New.

**Whole facility or one space?** Leave "Space" empty to close everything in
the building (fire alarm service, polling station, power cut). Pick a space to
close just that hall/room/pitch.

**Times** can be anything, they don't need to be on the half hour. Closing
07:40–11:10 is fine; any half-hour slot it touches becomes unavailable.

**Reason**: maintenance, event (council use), weather, staffing, other.
Weather is for pitches; the grounds team usually decide by 08:00 on the day.
(There's also a "kind" field that the screen fills in from the reason. Ignore
it.)

**Note**: short and polite — hirers see it in their cancellation message
("floor resurfacing", "polling station"). Don't put anything internal here.

### If there are bookings in the way

The closure is **not saved**; you get a list of who's affected instead.

1. Phone anyone affected, especially clubs (the email is not enough — they
   need to tell their members). Sometimes they'll take another hall instead;
   rebook them first.
2. Then save the closure again with **Cancel affected bookings** ticked.
   Everyone still in the closure gets a cancellation message and a full
   refund (or credit, for invoice customers). Block bookings lose those weeks;
   the series isn't extended, and the club gets one message listing the lost
   dates.

### Undoing a closure

Closures → the closure → Withdraw. The space becomes bookable again, **but
bookings that were cancelled because of it stay cancelled.** If the hirer still
wants the slot, rebook it for them by hand (and ask finance what to do about
the refund that's already gone out).

### Bank holidays

Enter them as facility-wide closures each January for the whole year. There's
no automatic bank-holiday list.
